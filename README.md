# Dissident Finance

![Dissident repositories](https://github.com/dissident-labs/.github/blob/main/Dissident%20Repositories.png?raw=true)

## 🧠 What is Dissident

**Dissident** is a next-gen investing app where users deploy their own AI agent to run personalized yield optimization strategies — without relying on advisors, funds, or platforms that take a cut.

Dissident gives investors the tools to transform their strategies into digital assets they fully own and control. By treating these AI-driven strategies as programmable and monetizable intellectual property, users can build a portfolio of agents that grow, evolve, and even generate revenue.  
Designed to be radically more intuitive than any alternative on the market, Dissident puts powerful automation and ownership into the hands of anyone — turning DeFi into a space for individual expression, creator-led finance, and true economic sovereignty.

---

## 🔥 How is Dissident Better?

Unlike robo-advisors, banks, or fund managers, Dissident allows users to **fully own both the agent and the strategy**.  
There are **no management, performance, or withdrawal fees** — just intelligent execution under complete user control.  
Dissident redefines investing as a transparent, self-directed experience.

---

## 💸 What Do Users Pay For?

Agents operate like vehicles on a decentralized highway — users only pay a fee when those agents perform an action.  
There are no hidden costs: **no management, performance, withdrawal, or load fees**.  
The pricing model is aligned with **real usage and value creation**.

---

## 🔐 Is Dissident Safe?

Dissident is designed to give users **full custody and visibility**.  
Assets remain in the user's wallet, strategies are user-owned, and every action taken by an agent is **transparent and permissioned**.  
Users remain **in control — always**.
